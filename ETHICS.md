# Ethics Statement

## Data subjects and consent

All measurement data in this repository was generated by the authors themselves on devices owned by the research team, using accounts created specifically for this study. No volunteers or other third parties were involved in the data generation, and no data of uninvolved persons was collected. The secondary user profiles created in the playbook are test profiles of the authors.

Smart scale measurements constitute health data and are special-category personal data under Art. 9 GDPR. The published data was therefore anonymized before release: email addresses, GPS coordinates, authentication and session tokens, and account names were replaced with placeholders in the companion app data and, in place, in the binary flash dumps (see `PROVENANCE.md`). No raw health data linked to identifiable individuals is included.

## Reported vulnerabilities

The paper reports two credential vulnerabilities in the Feelfit ecosystem: a non-rotating cloud authentication token and a database encryption key that is hard-coded identically across all installations. We contacted LIORQUE and Eufy about the findings affecting their ecosystems and gave each vendor a 90-day notice period as part of the disclosure process. Eufy classified the report as informative without immediate security impact, as attacks requiring physical access to a device are outside their program scope, and offered a reevaluation for a practical exploitation scenario. From a forensic perspective the finding remains relevant, as resale and disposal of a device constitute exactly such scenarios of physical access by third parties. LIORQUE has not responded to our reports.

The hard-coded database key is used by the claim scripts solely to decrypt the anonymized database copies included in this repository. This is a local, offline operation on data the authors own. Its inclusion adds no capability beyond what is already public, as the key is recoverable by a plain string search from any copy of the freely available APK.

## Expectations for evaluators

Evaluation of this artifact requires no interaction with vendor infrastructure. Evaluators should not replay tokens or credentials against vendor cloud APIs, register accounts for the purpose of probing the reported vulnerabilities, or otherwise interact with the vendors' services in the context of this evaluation. All claims are verifiable offline from the included data.

The companion app APKs are not redistributed in this repository for copyright reasons. They are available from the Google Play Store in the versions listed in `README.md`.

## Anonymity during artifact evaluation

This repository contains no analytics, tracking scripts, or other mechanisms that could reveal evaluator identities. The only network access in the evaluation workflow is `install.sh` fetching pinned helper scripts from the public Espressif ESP-IDF repository on GitHub.
